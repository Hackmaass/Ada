
# TalentGraph AI

> **Hire the engineer, not the resume.**

TalentGraph AI is an AI-native, evidence-driven talent intelligence platform that transforms technical work into trusted hiring intelligence.

---

# Meet ADA

## **A.D.A. — Adaptive Decision Assistant**

ADA is the intelligence layer powering the platform.

Instead of reading resumes, ADA continuously learns from evidence:

- GitHub
- Projects
- Hackathons
- Presentations
- AI Interviews
- Certifications
- Community Contributions

ADA builds a **Professional Digital Twin** for every candidate.

---

# Professional Digital Twin

A living, evolving representation of a candidate's professional capabilities.

Unlike a static resume, the Twin evolves every time new evidence is collected.

### Ask ADA

- Am I ready for a Senior Backend role?
- What should I learn next?
- Why was I ranked below another candidate?
- What happens if I spend 3 months learning Kubernetes?
- Which companies am I best suited for?

Every answer is explainable and backed by evidence.
