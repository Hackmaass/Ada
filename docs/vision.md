# Vision

## The Problem

Technical hiring is broken.

Recruiters spend an average of 6 seconds scanning a resume. That resume — a static, self-reported, unverified document — becomes the primary signal for one of the most expensive decisions a company makes.

The result:

- **Great engineers get overlooked** because they lack keyword-optimized resumes.
- **Mediocre candidates get hired** because they know how to game ATS systems.
- **Bias is amplified** through pattern matching on pedigree, not ability.
- **Hiring decisions are unexplainable** — no one can articulate *why* Candidate A was chosen over Candidate B.

Meanwhile, the actual evidence of engineering ability — repositories, architectures, commit histories, project complexity, open source contributions — sits in plain sight, completely ignored by hiring pipelines.

---

## Our Thesis

**The best predictor of future engineering performance is past engineering work.**

Not a resume. Not a LinkedIn headline. Not a 45-minute whiteboard session.

Real, verified, technical evidence.

---

## The TalentGraph AI Vision

TalentGraph AI replaces resume-based hiring with **evidence-driven hiring**.

We build a platform where:

1. **Candidates** are represented by a **Professional Digital Twin** — a living, evolving model of their capabilities built from verified evidence, not self-reported claims.

2. **Recruiters** interact with **ADA** (Adaptive Decision Assistant) — an AI intelligence layer that reasons over evidence, discovers candidates through natural language, and provides explainable hiring recommendations.

3. **Every recommendation is explainable.** No black boxes. No opaque scores. Every hiring decision can be traced back to specific evidence.

---

## Core Philosophy

```
Evidence → Knowledge → Intelligence → Decision
```

### Evidence First

Everything begins with evidence. We collect, verify, and structure technical work:

- GitHub repositories (code quality, architecture, contribution patterns)
- Resumes (claims to be verified, not trusted)
- Projects (complexity, technologies, outcomes)
- Hackathons (speed, creativity, collaboration)
- AI Interviews (communication, reasoning, depth)
- Presentations (knowledge sharing, teaching ability)
- Certifications (formal validation)
- Community Contributions (mentorship, open source)

### Knowledge Graph

Evidence is transformed into a structured knowledge representation — a graph of skills, projects, technologies, contributions, and relationships that captures the full professional landscape of a candidate.

### Professional Digital Twin

The knowledge graph powers a **Professional Digital Twin** — a continuously evolving model that represents what a candidate can actually do, not what they claim they can do.

### Explainable Intelligence

ADA reasons over the Digital Twin to provide explainable intelligence:

- Why is this candidate a strong fit?
- What specific evidence supports this recommendation?
- What are the candidate's actual strengths and gaps?
- How does this candidate compare to others — and why?

---

## What We Are NOT

- **Not another ATS.** We don't manage job postings or track applicants through a pipeline.
- **Not another resume builder.** We don't help candidates write better resumes — we make resumes irrelevant.
- **Not another chatbot.** ADA is an intelligence layer that reasons over structured evidence, not a wrapper around an LLM.
- **Not another skills assessment platform.** We don't administer tests — we analyze the work candidates have already done.

---

## What We ARE

An **AI-native operating system for technical hiring** that:

- Transforms raw technical work into structured intelligence
- Builds living professional models of every candidate
- Enables natural language candidate discovery
- Provides explainable, evidence-backed hiring decisions
- Helps candidates understand and grow their capabilities

---

## Long-Term Vision

Build the **world's trust layer for technical talent**.

A future where:

- Every engineer has a verifiable, portable professional identity
- Hiring decisions are transparent, fair, and evidence-based
- Career growth is guided by data, not guesswork
- The gap between "what you can do" and "what people think you can do" disappears

---

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over claims.** Never trust self-reported data without verification.
2. **Explainability over accuracy.** A recommendation that cannot be explained is worthless.
3. **Continuous over static.** Professional models must evolve with every new piece of evidence.
4. **Fair over fast.** Bias reduction is more important than speed.
5. **Open over opaque.** Candidates should always understand how they are evaluated.
